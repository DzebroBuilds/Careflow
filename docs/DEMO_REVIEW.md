# CareFlow friend-review demo

Share this entry point after deployment:
https://dzebrobuilds.github.io/Careflow/demo/start/

No account is required. All examples are fictional. Each visitor's sample edits stay in their browser; visitors do not share a live workspace or access the owner's private test account.

## Walkthrough

1. Open the dashboard, switch houses, and open an attention task.
2. Open Staff, add a fictional employee, and assign a home. Activate the profile if you want to schedule it.
3. Open Shifts and cover the unassigned shift. Try overlapping assignments to see validation.
4. Open Time approvals, approve Jordan's sample time, or submit new sample hours.
5. Open Expenses and approve a sample purchase or enter a fictional expense.
6. Open Payroll preview and download the approved-hours CSV. No payroll provider receives it.
7. Refresh a page to confirm your sample edits remain. Access & settings -> Reset sample workspace restores the fictional examples and clears operations edits.

## What to ask your reviewer

- Was it clear which home you were working in?
- Could you find staff, shift coverage, resident documents and approvals?
- Did any button or label leave you unsure what happened?
- Which daily task should we improve next?

## Scope

These are interactive sample workflows, not production staff scheduling, financial processing, payroll execution, regulatory certification or clinical decision support. The private Intuit sandbox connection remains separate from the public visitor demo. No real resident, employee or financial information should be entered.
